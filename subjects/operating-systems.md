# Operating Systems

## Topics

- Processes, threads, process states, and context switching.
- CPU scheduling and performance measures.
- Concurrency, critical sections, locks, semaphores, and communication.
- Deadlocks: conditions, prevention, avoidance, and recovery.
- Memory allocation, paging, virtual memory, and page replacement.
- Filesystems, permissions, I/O, and system calls.

## Practice

Compare processes and threads, trace a scheduling example, identify a race condition, and explain what happens during a page fault.

## Connection to the goals

Operating systems commonly appear in systems interviews and are a core subject for teacher-exam preparation. Focus on explaining behavior with small examples, not memorizing terms alone.
