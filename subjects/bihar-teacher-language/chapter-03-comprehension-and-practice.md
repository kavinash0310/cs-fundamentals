# Chapter 3 — Comprehension and Language Practice

## 1. A repeatable passage method

1. Read the question stem first to know whether it asks for a fact, inference, title, meaning, or sequence.
2. Read the passage once for its main claim and again for evidence.
3. Underline contrast words such as however, although, लेकिन, फिर भी.
4. Reject an option that broadens the passage's time, people, or cause.
5. For vocabulary, replace the word in the sentence and test whether the meaning still fits.

A passage can report two events without claiming that one caused the other. An inference must be supported by the given text; outside knowledge does not repair missing evidence.

## 2. Sentence ordering

Use pronouns and reference words first: “this result” needs an earlier result; “they” needs a plural antecedent. Then use chronology and cause. Finally read the entire order aloud for coherence.

Example:
A. “This reduced repeated failures.”
B. “The team found that a loose cable caused the shutdown.”
C. “It secured the cable and retested the system.”

Correct order: B → C → A. It refers to the team's action; This refers to the retest outcome.

## 3. Mixed language mini-test

Choose the best answer. For practice, allow 20 minutes; then check explanations.

1. Each of the files (is/are) backed up.
2. Correct the sentence: “She has called me yesterday.”
3. Choose an article: “___ honest answer.”
4. Choose the preposition: “depend (at/on) evidence.”
5. Correct: “Although the test was easy, but it took time.”
6. In “The explanation was remarkably clear,” what does remarkably modify?
7. What is the best inference from: “The attendance rose in March, but the passage gives no reason”?
8. Arrange: (A) “Therefore, the team revised the schedule.” (B) “The first plan allowed too little setup time.” (C) “Several classes started late.”
9. “लड़कियाँ पुस्तक ___।” (पढ़ता हैं / पढ़ती हैं)
10. “राजपुत्र” का विग्रह क्या है?
11. “वह धीरे बोलता है” में “धीरे” कौन-सा शब्द-भेद है?
12. “माता-पिता” किस समास का उदाहरण है?
13. “नाक कटना” का अर्थ चुनें: (अ) घायल होना (आ) अपमान होना (इ) प्रसन्न होना.
14. “वे कल आएँगे” किस काल का उदाहरण है?
15. अशुद्धि सुधारें: “प्रत्येक बच्चे खेलते हैं।”
16. गद्यांश: “विद्यालय ने पुस्तकालय का समय बढ़ाया। अगले महीने पुस्तक निर्गमन बढ़ा, पर सर्वेक्षण में यह नहीं पूछा गया कि वृद्धि का कारण क्या था।” क्या निष्कर्ष समर्थित है?
17. Passage title should usually describe what?
18. If an option says “all students improved” while the passage describes one class, what is the problem?
19. वाक्य में क्रिया किससे मेल खाती है, यह जाँचने का पहला कदम क्या है?
20. A sentence begins with “However.” What relationship should you look for?

## 4. Answers with explanations

1. is; each is singular.
2. “She called me yesterday.” A finished past time takes simple past.
3. An; honest begins with a vowel sound.
4. on; the standard phrase is depend on.
5. “Although the test was easy, it took time.” Do not pair although with but.
6. Clear, an adjective; remarkably is an adverb.
7. Attendance rose; its cause is not given.
8. B → C → A. The inadequate setup time explains the late starts; Therefore introduces the response to that problem.
9. पढ़ती हैं; plural feminine subject.
10. राजा का पुत्र; तत्पुरुष समास.
11. क्रिया-विशेषण; it describes how he speaks.
12. द्वंद्व; both parents are included.
13. आ; अपमान होना।
14. भविष्यत् काल।
15. “प्रत्येक बच्चा खेलता है” (or “प्रत्येक बच्ची खेलती है” when the referent is feminine); प्रत्येक is singular.
16. Book borrowing increased after the schedule change, but the passage does not establish that the change caused it.
17. The central topic or claim supported by the whole passage.
18. It overgeneralizes beyond the described class.
19. Identify the head subject and number/person before choosing the verb.
20. A contrast with the previous statement.
