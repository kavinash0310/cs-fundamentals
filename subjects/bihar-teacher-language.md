# Bihar Teacher Language Preparation

Use this course for objective English and Hindi language practice. It teaches grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, and comprehension. Check the current BPSC notice for the languages offered, whether a language paper is qualifying, and its exact question count and cutoff.

## Study method

1. Choose the language option allowed by the current notice.
2. Read the whole sentence before selecting an answer; grammar is about relationships, not isolated words.
3. For comprehension, answer from the passage rather than outside assumptions.
4. Keep separate error lists for English and Hindi and repeat missed rules after a delay.

This repository now has introductory English, Hindi, Urdu, and Bengali modules. The shorter Urdu/Bengali chapters are foundations; deepen them with the current prescribed textbook and language syllabus. The [official 2024 BPSC teacher booklet](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/BPSC_content/Notices/NB-2024-08-09-19.pdf) illustrates a historical qualifying-language section with common English items and a choice among Hindi, Urdu, or Bengali; future notices may change the pattern.
