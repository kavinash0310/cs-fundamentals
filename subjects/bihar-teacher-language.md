# Bihar Teacher Language Preparation

Use this course for objective English, Hindi, Urdu, and Bengali language foundations. It teaches grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, script-aware reading, and comprehension; English and Hindi currently have the fuller shared practice sequence. Check the current BPSC notice for the languages offered, whether a language paper is qualifying, and its exact question count and cutoff.

## Study method

1. Choose the language option allowed by the current notice.
2. Read the whole sentence before selecting an answer; grammar is about relationships, not isolated words.
3. For comprehension, answer from the passage rather than outside assumptions.
4. Keep an error list for each language you select and repeat missed rules after a delay.
5. Attempt the [four-language practice set](../practice/bihar-teacher-language-practice-01.md); solve only the language sections relevant to your notice.

This repository now has chaptered English/Hindi preparation and two Urdu/Bengali chapters apiece. Urdu and Bengali have less total coverage; deepen them with the current prescribed textbook and language syllabus. The [official 2024 BPSC teacher booklet](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/BPSC_content/Notices/NB-2024-08-09-19.pdf) illustrates a historical qualifying-language section with common English items and a choice among Hindi, Urdu, or Bengali; future notices may change the pattern.
