# GATE General Aptitude

A four-chapter course for the common GATE aptitude section. The 2027 paper pattern assigns General Aptitude 15 marks; the official GA syllabus includes verbal, quantitative, analytical, and spatial aptitude.

## Chapter sequence

| Chapter | Coverage |
|---|---|
| [1. Verbal aptitude](general-aptitude/chapter-01-verbal-aptitude.md) | Tenses, articles, adjectives, parts of speech, agreement, prepositions, conjunctions, vocabulary, idioms, reading, sequencing |
| [2. Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation](general-aptitude/chapter-02-quantitative-aptitude.md) | Ratios, percentages, rates, powers, exponents, logarithms, counting, series, geometry, mensuration, statistics, probability, charts, plots, maps |
| [3. Analytical and spatial reasoning](general-aptitude/chapter-03-analytical-and-spatial-reasoning.md) | Deduction, induction, analogy, numerical relations, 2D/3D transformations, assembling, grouping, patterns, paper folding and cutting |
| [4. Timed practice and error review](general-aptitude/chapter-04-timed-practice-and-error-review.md) | Mixed questions, time strategy, error diagnosis and spaced practice |

Follow the current official [GATE General Aptitude syllabus](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/GA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf) and question-paper pattern. This course covers the syllabus categories; continued timed practice is needed to build speed.
