# Bihar Teacher General Studies

This course teaches broad Bihar and Indian General Studies foundations through eight extended lessons with maps, worked calculations, diagrams, source-checking methods, and explained questions. It is a preparation framework, not an assertion that every topic or paper split below is prescribed for every recruitment cycle. BPSC notices and corrigenda control.

## How to use this course

1. Read the current official notice and mark only the relevant paper and teaching level.
2. Learn the stable concepts from these chapters; verify changing figures, schemes, office holders, and exam rules from official sources.
3. Keep a dated current-affairs notebook and record the source for every fact.
4. Solve the linked official previous paper under its printed time and marking rules. Do not assume an old paper's pattern carries into a new cycle.

The official 2023 Higher Secondary Paper 4 is a historical example: its booklet says 40 General Studies questions and 80 Computer Science questions, 120 marks in two hours, with a one-fourth penalty for a wrong answer. See the [official question booklet](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/Archive/2023/NB-2023-08-26-37.pdf) and [provisional key](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in/Archive/2023/NB-2023-09-01-40.pdf). The current BPSC notice may prescribe a different structure.
