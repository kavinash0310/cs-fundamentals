# Chapter 6 — Quantitative and Mental Ability

This chapter adapts arithmetic and reasoning for objective General Studies questions. For fuller derivations, use the [GATE General Aptitude course](../general-aptitude.md).

## 1. Arithmetic toolkit

- Percentage = part/whole × 100. A rise from 80 to 100 is 20/80=25%.
- Ratio 2:3 with total 50 has five parts; each part is 10, so the values are 20 and 30.
- Average = total/count. For rates, add work per unit time, not completion times.
- Simple interest: SI=principal × rate × time/100, with consistent time units.
- Speed=distance/time; average speed over a trip is total distance divided by total time, not the average of the segment speeds unless times are equal.
- For profit/loss percentage, identify whether the base is cost price or selling price before calculating.

**Worked trip:** Travel 120 km at 90 km/h and 80 km at 40 km/h. Time = 120/90 + 80/40 = 4/3 + 2 = 10/3 hours = 3 hours 20 minutes. Average speed = 200/(10/3)=60 km/h.

## 2. Number sense and data

Estimate before exact calculation. Check units, denominator, and scale. In a bar chart, compare actual values, not visual height alone. In a table, sum only the requested rows. For a pie chart, sector percentage=angle/360×100.

If the axis begins at 90 and bars are 95 and 100, their true difference is 5. A truncated axis can exaggerate that gap visually.

## 3. Reasoning patterns

Deduction asks what must follow from given facts; induction proposes a general rule from examples; analogy matches a relationship. For syllogisms, draw sets and avoid adding unstated facts. In a sequence, test differences, ratios, alternating patterns, squares, and interleaving; finite examples may allow more than one rule.

For direction questions, keep a coordinate sketch. For arrangements, translate each sentence into a constraint before trying orders. For spatial questions, track whether an operation translates, rotates, reflects, or scales the figure.

## 4. Practice with solutions

1. A price rises 20% from ₹500. New price? ₹500×1.2=₹600.
2. A price rises 20% then falls 20%. Net? 1.2×0.8=0.96, so a 4% decrease.
3. 5 workers finish in 12 days. At equal rates, 10 workers need? 60 worker-days/10=6 days.
4. All A are B; no B are C. Can A be C? No.
5. Complete 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, __. Differences 3,5,7,9; next 11, so 37 under the simple pattern.
6. A fair coin is tossed twice. Probability of exactly one head? HT and TH out of four outcomes: 1/2.

Keep an error log: concept, mistaken assumption, corrected method, and next review date.

## 5. Extended objective-math toolkit

### Percentages, profit, discount, and interest

Use the original/base value as denominator. If a value changes successively by rates a and b, multiply factors:

~~~text
new value = original × (1 + a/100) × (1 + b/100)
~~~

A 10% increase then a 20% decrease gives 1.10 × 0.80 = 0.88, so the final value is 12% lower, not 10% lower. If cost price is ₹800 and selling price ₹920, profit is ₹120 and profit percentage = 120/800 × 100 = **15%**. A 20% discount on marked price ₹1,000 gives selling price ₹800; do not confuse discount percentage with profit percentage.

Simple interest = PRT/100. Compound amount for annual compounding = P(1 + r/100)^t. For ₹10,000 at 5% simple interest for 2 years, interest = ₹1,000. At annual compound interest, amount = 10,000×1.05² = ₹11,025.

### Ratio, mixture, and average

For ratio 3:5 and total 64, one part is 64/8 = 8, so quantities are 24 and 40. A weighted average uses weights:

~~~text
weighted mean = sum(value × frequency) / sum(frequency)
~~~

If 20 students average 60 and 30 average 70, combined average = (20×60 + 30×70)/50 = 66.

### Work, pipes, and motion

Rates add when people work together. A completes a task in 12 days, B in 18 days: combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 task/day, so time = 36/5 = **7.2 days**. For travel, average speed is total distance / total time. A 150 km journey at 50 km/h takes 3 hours; another 150 km at 75 km/h takes 2 hours. Average speed = 300/5 = **60 km/h**, not 62.5.

### Algebra, geometry, and probability reminders

- Linear equation: perform the same operation on both sides. From 3x − 5 = 16, x = 7.
- Rectangle area = length×breadth; perimeter = 2(length+breadth). Circle area = πr²; circumference = 2πr.
- Triangle angle sum = 180° in Euclidean plane geometry. A right triangle obeys a²+b²=c².
- Probability = favorable equally likely outcomes / total outcomes; verify that outcomes are equally likely before using this shortcut.
- For a fair die, probability of an even number = 3/6 = 1/2.

### Data interpretation checklist

1. Read title, unit, scale, and date before values.
2. Check whether a graph begins at zero; a truncated axis can visually exaggerate differences.
3. Convert percent to count only with the correct total.
4. Compare like periods and like definitions.
5. Estimate first; a result outside the chart's range is a signal to recheck.

### Solved practice

1. A number falls from 250 to 200. **Decrease = 50/250 ×100 = 20%.**
2. A ₹2,000 item gets 15% off. **Discount ₹300; selling price ₹1,700.**
3. 12 machines make 600 items in 5 hours. At equal productivity, 8 machines in 5 hours make **600×8/12 = 400 items.**
4. A person walks 3 km east and 4 km north. Straight-line displacement is **√(3²+4²)=5 km northeast.**
5. Solve 5x + 7 = 32. **5x=25, x=5.**
6. A rectangle is 12 m by 7 m. **Area 84 m²; perimeter 38 m.**
7. A fair die is rolled once. Probability of a result greater than 4? **2/6=1/3.**
8. Values 4, 7, 7, 12: mean? **30/4=7.5; median is (7+7)/2=7.**
9. A 40 L mixture is 25% juice. How much juice? **0.25×40=10 L.**
10. A map distance is 3 cm at 1:500,000. **3×5 km = 15 km.**
11. A 30% rise from 100 followed by a 30% fall gives? **100×1.3×0.7=91, a 9% net fall.**
12. A train covers 180 km in 3 h. **Average speed 60 km/h.**

For every answer, write the base, unit, and one reason the most tempting wrong option is wrong.
