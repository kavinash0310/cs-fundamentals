# Engineering Mathematics and Discrete Mathematics

This course covers the Engineering Mathematics and core discrete-structure topics listed for GATE CS 2027: logic, sets, relations, partial orders and lattices, monoids and groups, graph theory, counting, recurrence relations, generating functions, linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics.

## Chapter sequence

| Chapter | Coverage |
|---|---|
| [1. Logic, sets, relations, and proof](discrete-mathematics/chapter-01-logic-sets-relations-and-proofs.md) | Propositional and first-order logic, sets/functions, equivalence and order relations, induction |
| [2. Counting, recurrences, and generating functions](discrete-mathematics/chapter-02-counting-recurrences-and-generating-functions.md) | Product/sum rules, permutations/combinations, inclusion-exclusion, recurrences, generating functions |
| [3. Graphs, orders, and algebraic structures](discrete-mathematics/chapter-03-graphs-orders-and-algebraic-structures.md) | Connectivity, trees, matching, coloring, partial orders, lattices, monoids, groups |
| [4. Linear algebra](discrete-mathematics/chapter-04-linear-algebra.md) | Matrices, determinants, linear systems, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, LU |
| [5. Calculus](discrete-mathematics/chapter-05-calculus.md) | Limits, continuity, derivatives, extrema, mean value theorem, integration |
| [6. Probability and statistics](discrete-mathematics/chapter-06-probability-and-statistics.md) | Conditional probability, Bayes, random variables, distributions, expectation, spread, descriptive statistics |

## How to study

Keep proof steps explicit. For calculation questions, show intermediate work and verify a result by substitution or a second method. Draw relation/Hasse diagrams, graph examples, matrices, probability trees, and function sketches by hand. Then solve the questions without looking at the answer key.

The calculus and probability chapters provide the GATE CS level foundation; they are not a complete mathematics degree course. Check the current official [GATE CS syllabus](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf) for your attempt.
