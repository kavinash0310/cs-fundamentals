# Discrete Mathematics

## Topics

- Propositional logic, predicates, implication, and proof techniques.
- Sets, relations, functions, equivalence, and partial orders.
- Counting, permutations, combinations, and the pigeonhole principle.
- Recurrences, induction, and basic asymptotic reasoning.
- Graphs and trees; connectivity, paths, and traversals.
- Elementary probability used in algorithm analysis.

## Practice

Prove small claims by induction, count arrangements, simplify logical statements, and translate a word problem into sets, relations, or a graph.

## Connection to the goals

Discrete math supports algorithms and theoretical CS. It is usually a supporting area in interviews and a useful foundation for teacher-exam questions.
