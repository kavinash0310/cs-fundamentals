# Computer Fundamentals and ICT — Course Index

This course has been expanded from a short outline into a seven-chapter learning sequence. Start with Chapter 1 and move in order. Each chapter includes explanations, labelled drawings or diagrams, worked examples, questions with answers, and a self-check.

## How to study

1. Read a section once for meaning; then write the key definition in your own words.
2. Copy and redraw each diagram by hand without looking.
3. Rework every numerical example with the solution covered.
4. Attempt the short-answer and MCQ questions before opening the answers.
5. Keep an error log: record the mistaken idea, corrected rule, and one new example.
6. Revisit the error log after one day, one week, and before a mock test.

## Exam coverage and boundaries

### GATE CS

The official GATE CS 2027 syllabus names separate sections for Digital Logic, Computer Organization and Architecture, Operating Systems, and Computer Networks. These chapters teach the shared foundations and selected GATE-level calculations; the linked courses below must be studied for full section coverage.

- Number representation and arithmetic: Chapter 2, then [Digital Logic](digital-logic.md) for Boolean algebra, minimization, and circuit design.
- CPU, addressing, pipelines, cache, interrupts, and DMA: Chapters 3–4, then [Computer Organization and Architecture](computer-organization-architecture.md) for full architecture depth.
- OS introduction: Chapter 5, then [Operating Systems](operating-systems.md).
- Networking introduction: Chapter 6, then [Computer Networks](computer-networks.md).
- The official syllabus also includes Engineering Mathematics, Programming and Data Structures, Algorithms, Theory of Computation, Compiler Design, and Databases. Use the complete subject map in the repository README; this course alone is not the full GATE curriculum.

### Bihar computer-teacher exams

Computer basics, digital representation, hardware, software, operating-system concepts, networks, ICT, and classroom use are useful foundations. Exam level and exact syllabus differ by recruitment notification. The BPSC syllabus page and the notice for the exact post and teaching level take priority over this broad course. Historical papers are for practice, not proof that the next exam has the same syllabus.
