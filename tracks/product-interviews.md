# Product Company Interview Track

Use this track with the subject courses. Interview loops vary by company, role, and level.

## Preparation areas

1. **Coding:** Choose a language and practice its collections, strings, standard library, debugging, and edge cases.
2. **DSA:** Work through arrays, linked structures, trees, heaps, hashing, graphs, greedy methods, and dynamic programming.
3. **CS fundamentals:** Explain databases, operating systems, networking, computer organization, and programming concepts with examples.
4. **Object design:** Clarify requirements, responsibilities, interfaces, invariants, and testability.
5. **System design:** For roles that require it, estimate load, draw request/data flow, and discuss reliability, consistency, security, and trade-offs.
6. **Communication:** Clarify constraints, state a simple solution, prove the key idea, analyze time/space, test boundaries, and compare alternatives.

## Practice loop

- Solve before reading the solution.
- Write down the invariant and complexity.
- Test normal, boundary, and failure cases.
- Explain the solution aloud as if teaching it.
- Revisit missed questions after a few days.
- Build small projects to connect fundamentals to working software.
