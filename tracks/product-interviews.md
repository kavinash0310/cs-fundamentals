# Product Company Interview Track

Use this track alongside the subject notes. The exact interview loop varies by company, role, and level.

## Preparation areas

1. **Coding:** Pick one interview language and get comfortable with its standard library, collections, strings, sorting, and debugging.
2. **DSA:** Practice arrays and strings, hash maps, linked lists, stacks and queues, trees, heaps, graphs, recursion, backtracking, greedy methods, and dynamic programming.
3. **CS fundamentals:** Be ready to explain databases, operating systems, networking, and OOP in your own words and apply them to examples.
4. **Design:** For experienced roles, practice object/API design and distributed-system design. Explain requirements, data model, interfaces, scaling choices, and trade-offs.
5. **Communication:** Clarify the problem, describe a simple approach first, analyze complexity, test edge cases, and discuss alternatives.

## Practice loop

- Solve a problem without looking at a solution first.
- Write down the key idea and time/space complexity.
- Test boundary cases and explain the solution aloud.
- Revisit problems you could not solve after a few days.
- Build small projects to connect fundamentals to working software.
